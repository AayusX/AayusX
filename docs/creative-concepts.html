Recommended direction

02 · Liquid Atlas

It is the strongest balance of a signature purple world, an original cursor companion, and a liquid type moment that belongs to the person rather than to a generic tech aesthetic.

  • Changa, the kite, is personal to Nepal and creates a natural cursor tether.
  • The dark opening gives way to a light, readable world; the contrast is memorable without making every section dark.
  • The liquid effect can be built with CSS and SVG filters, with a solid text layer and a fallback.
  • The mascot can be a lightweight SVG with a static mobile state and a visible off switch.

Next: apply this direction to the real shell, starting with the opening, hero, mascot, and one project composition before rolling it through every section.

Night Signal is the most cinematic and the most purely purple, but it risks reading as a familiar space/tech atmosphere. Paper Circuit is the most tactile and the closest to the current warm identity, but it is less of a departure and less liquid. Liquid Atlas is the direction that best earns the words “distinctive, personal, and alive.”
